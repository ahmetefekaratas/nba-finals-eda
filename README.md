# NBA Finals & MVP Exploratory Data Analysis (1951–2018)

An Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) investigating historical NBA Finals outcomes and Regular Season MVP records.

## Project Overview
This project examines the relationship between individual player accolades and championship success, historical franchise dynasties, series competitiveness, and player attributes over nearly seven decades of NBA history.

## Key Insights
- **1. MVP Positions:** Centers and Forwards historically won the most MVP awards, showing the value of big men in that era.
- **2. MVP Physical Profiles:** The average height was around 2.05 meters, proving that the league strongly rewarded tall and versatile athletes.
- **3. Regular Season MVP vs. Title:** More than 51% of regular season MVPs did not reach the Finals, showing that individual awards do not guarantee a championship.
- **4. Franchise Dominance:** Titles were mostly won by top dynasties, led mainly by the Boston Celtics and Los Angeles Lakers.
- **5. Finals Competitiveness:** Over 63% of Finals series went to 6 or 7 games, while clean 4-0 sweeps were very rare at only 13.24%.
- **6. Geographic Origins:** US players won 94.1% of MVP trophies, showing an almost total American dominance throughout this era.
